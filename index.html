HLS format switch test

Plays an HLS stream through hls.js and forces quality switches between video formats (H.264, HEVC, VP9, AV1). It logs hls.js errors and flags the moment the picture stops while playback time keeps moving.

Format cycle: every 6 seconds it asks hls.js to move to the next format at the same resolution, the way Auto can when the connection speed changes. Press play first.
